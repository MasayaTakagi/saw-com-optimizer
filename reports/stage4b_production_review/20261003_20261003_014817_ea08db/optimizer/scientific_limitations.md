# Scientific limitations

- Results are relative to the current provisional MLIT COM profile and existing hybrid/resonator model.
- They do not establish real Band 28 feasibility, calibrated physical prediction, a real-device optimum, a final topology optimum, or a final impedance recommendation.
- Stage 4A smoke results are implementation evidence only and are excluded from scientific ranking.
- The P_D/D_P warning is a descriptive paired-seed diagnostic; it is not proof of physical symmetry failure.
